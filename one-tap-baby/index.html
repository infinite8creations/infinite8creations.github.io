One Tap Baby

A simple, private baby log for both parents, made for the middle of the night. No sign-up in the app, no ads, no tracking: your log stays in your own iCloud, and nothing is sent to us.

Download on the App Store

Why parents choose it

  • Private. No sign-up in the app, no ads, no tracking. Your log lives in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours.
  • Made for 3 a.m. One hand, big buttons, and the time of the last feed first.
  • One log for both parents. Both iPhones see the same log, and the second parent never pays.
  • Pay once, keep it for good. No subscription.
  • Mixed bottles. Breast milk and formula in the same bottle.
  • Twins and siblings. Each baby has their own log and color.
  • Ready for the pediatrician. A clear PDF report, in a few taps.

One Tap Baby is on the App Store in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries. It is not yet available in the European Union.

What it does

  • The first screen answers the night question: when did the baby last eat? Four big buttons sit within thumb reach.
  • Bottle: breast milk, formula, or both in one bottle; your last bottle is pre-filled. Breastfeeding timer, left and right.
  • Diaper: one tap, it's saved. Sleep: start it now, or log the night when you wake up.
  • Forgot to log it? Add it afterwards, or fix any entry.
  • Both parents see the same log on their own iPhone. One purchase unlocks it for both: the second parent never pays.
  • Next-feed reminder, 1.5 to 4 hours after the last feed. mL or oz.
  • Twins and siblings, each with their own log and color.
  • Today in three tiles, the last 7 days as a chart, and a PDF report for your pediatrician.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?
No One Tap Baby account. Each parent needs an iCloud account on their iPhone to share the log.

How much does One Tap Baby cost?
It is free to try for 7 days, then a one-time purchase ($9.99 in the US). There is no subscription and no ads.

Does it work on Android?
No. One Tap Baby is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later).
