A baby tracker for both parents. The second one never pays.

Whoever does the 3 a.m. bottle, the other parent sees it in the morning on their own iPhone. One Tap Baby syncs through your own iCloud: there is no account to create in the app.

Download on the App Store

Why parents choose it

  • Free for the second parent. One purchase covers the whole family.
  • Pay once, keep it for good. No subscription, no ads.
  • Made for 3 a.m. One hand, big buttons, and the time of the last feed first.
  • Mixed bottles. Breast milk and formula in the same bottle.
  • Twins and siblings. Each baby has their own log and color.
  • Ready for the pediatrician. A clear PDF report, in a few taps.
  • Private. Your log lives in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours.

One Tap Baby is on the App Store in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries. It is not yet available in the European Union.

How sharing works

  • Invite the other parent from Settings. All your babies are shared in one invitation.
  • Each entry shows who logged it.
  • Optional alert when the other parent logs: off by default, filtered by feeds, diapers or sleep, and silent.
  • The second parent never pays: one purchase unlocks the app for both of you.
  • See who has access, remove someone, or stop sharing anytime. If sharing stops, each parent keeps a copy: nothing is erased.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later for each parent, each signed in to iCloud. No sign-up in the app: it uses the iCloud account already on your iPhone.

Private by design

Your log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. One Tap Baby has no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sent to us.

Questions

Is it free for the second parent?
Yes. The parent you invite never pays: one purchase unlocks the app for both parents sharing the log.

Do I need to create an account?
No account in the app. Both parents need to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone to share the log.
