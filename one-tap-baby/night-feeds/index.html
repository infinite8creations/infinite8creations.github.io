A one-tap baby tracker for night feeds

At 3 a.m. you need one answer: when did the baby last eat? One Tap Baby shows it at the top of the first screen, with four big buttons at the bottom, within thumb reach.

Download on the App Store

Why parents choose it

  • Private. No sign-up in the app, no ads, no tracking. Your log lives in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours.
  • Made for 3 a.m. One hand, big buttons, and the time of the last feed first.
  • One log for both parents. Both iPhones see the same log, and the second parent never pays.
  • Pay once, keep it for good. No subscription.
  • Mixed bottles. Breast milk and formula in the same bottle.
  • Twins and siblings. Each baby has their own log and color.
  • Ready for the pediatrician. A clear PDF report, in a few taps.

One Tap Baby is on the App Store in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries. It is not yet available in the European Union.

Fast, even half asleep

  • Bottle: breast milk, formula, or both in one bottle. Your last bottle is pre-filled, so it can be just Bottle, then Done.
  • Breastfeeding: one tap starts the timer on the chosen side, one tap switches sides, Done saves it.
  • Diaper: one tap, it's saved.
  • Sleep: tap when the baby falls asleep, or log the whole night when you wake up.

Calm in the dark

  • Each baby has their own color, and the app has a dark mode.
  • The next-feed reminder rings after the delay you choose, from 1.5 to 4 hours after the last feed.
  • If the other parent logs a feed, you can get a silent alert, with no sound.

Forgot to log it?

Add it afterwards, at the right time, or fix any entry. A night logged in the morning counts for the day you woke up.

Questions

Can I log a feed with one hand?
Yes. The four buttons are at the bottom of the screen, within thumb reach, and a diaper is saved in one tap.

Can I log a feed I forgot?
Yes. Add it afterwards at the right time, never in the future, or fix any entry.
