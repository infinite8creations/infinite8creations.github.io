A Huckleberry alternative without a subscription

Huckleberry is a rich app with paid plans, including a sleep improvement plan. If you only need to log feeds, diapers and sleep, together, without a subscription, One Tap Baby is a smaller option: free for 7 days, then a one-time purchase.

Download on the App Store

Why parents choose it

  • Free for the second parent. One purchase covers the whole family.
  • Pay once, keep it for good. No subscription, no ads.
  • Made for 3 a.m. One hand, big buttons, and the time of the last feed first.
  • Mixed bottles. Breast milk and formula in the same bottle.
  • Twins and siblings. Each baby has their own log and color.
  • Ready for the pediatrician. A clear PDF report, in a few taps.
  • Private. Your log lives in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours.

One Tap Baby is on the App Store in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries. It is not yet available in the European Union.

Choose Huckleberry if

You want its paid sleep features and plans, an iPad or Apple Watch app, or an app with tens of thousands of ratings.

Choose One Tap Baby if

You want a fast log for the night, shared by both parents on iPhone, with no subscription and nothing sent to a server of ours.

Questions

Is there a Huckleberry alternative with no subscription?
One Tap Baby logs feeds, diapers and sleep for both parents with a one-time purchase after a free 7-day trial ($9.99 in the US).

Does One Tap Baby give sleep advice?
No. It records what happened; it gives no advice and sets no targets.
