A baby tracker with no subscription

One Tap Baby is free to try for 7 days, then a one-time purchase: $9.99 in the US. No subscription, no ads, and one purchase unlocks the app for both parents.

Download on the App Store

Why parents choose it

  • Free for the second parent. One purchase covers the whole family.
  • Pay once, keep it for good. No subscription, no ads.
  • Made for 3 a.m. One hand, big buttons, and the time of the last feed first.
  • Mixed bottles. Breast milk and formula in the same bottle.
  • Twins and siblings. Each baby has their own log and color.
  • Ready for the pediatrician. A clear PDF report, in a few taps.
  • Private. Your log lives in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours.

One Tap Baby is on the App Store in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries. It is not yet available in the European Union.

How the price works

  • Download the app for free. The 7-day trial starts when you choose it, and its length is shown before it begins.
  • You can unlock it during the trial or after. Prices are set country by country by the App Store; your price appears in your currency.
  • Share the app with another parent during your trial and you get a lower price.
  • Restore purchases on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account.

If you don't buy

Nothing is deleted. After the trial, your log stays readable and you can still export it; you just can't add new entries.

Questions

Is One Tap Baby a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase after a free 7-day trial ($9.99 in the US).

What happens when the trial ends?
Nothing is deleted. The log stays readable and exportable; adding new entries needs the one-time unlock.
